Writing tips
  • Write names with a capital letter, like jan Mali. Each sound gets a glyph inside a cartouche.
  • Or build a cartouche yourself: [mama ala lipu ilo]
  • Join two glyphs with &, stack them with -, or scale them with +: toki&pona
  • Add 1 after a word for its alternate glyph, or a higher number where there are more: mute1, sewi1, ni3
  • Put the words a long glyph covers in parentheses: pi(toki pona), (tenpo ni) la
  • In a cartouche, a comma after a glyph reads a syllable instead of the first letter: [pona] is P, [pona,] is Po, [pona,,] is Pona.
  • Each sentence ending in ., ! or ? starts a new line in the image. Turn that off under Lines, or start a new line in the text yourself.
  • Tap a glyph in the preview to see which word it is.

How do I write sitelen pona on my phone?

Type toki pona in the box above. ilo sitelen draws it in sitelen pona and gives you an image to paste into WhatsApp, Discord, Telegram or any chat. There is no font or keyboard to install, and it works offline once added to your home screen. It converts toki pona to sitelen pona, it does not translate: you write the toki pona, it draws the glyphs from the nasin nanpa font.
