Sending a changed message: three behaviours

You open a message from Recents, change a word and send it again. What should happen to the card? Try each one with the same steps and pick by feel. All three share the same Recents.

A. Ask on send

Copy turns into two buttons, “Copy and replace” and “Copy as new”. Nothing is sent until you choose.

  1. Open a message from Recents and change a word.
  2. Tap Copy. Pick one of the two buttons.
  3. Do it again and tap somewhere else instead: the question goes away.
Try A

B. Ask on leaving

Copy always copies at once and leaves Recents alone. When you tap New or another card, a dialog asks: save the changes, save as new, or discard.

  1. Open a message from Recents and change a word.
  2. Tap Copy. Look at Recents: the card still shows the old text.
  3. Tap New, or another card. Answer the dialog.
  4. Do it again, copy, then close the tab and come back: the question is still waiting.
Try B

C. Quiet, with “Keep both”

Copy always copies at once and updates the card you opened. The toast stays up for seven seconds with a “Keep both” button that brings the old card back beside the new one.

  1. Open a message from Recents and change a word.
  2. Tap Copy. The card now shows the new text.
  3. Do it again and tap “Keep both” in the toast.
Try C

In all three: sending the same text again just moves the card to the top, a brand new message becomes a card on its first send, and New or an emptied box starts a separate message. Over plain http on the home network, Copy and Share are not allowed by the browser, so use Save there, or the https preview.
